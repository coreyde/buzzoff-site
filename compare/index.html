Buzz Off helps you quit or cut back on caffeine. Here’s how it compares with two other quit apps and three caffeine trackers, using only what each app’s own App Store page says.

Where other apps go further

  • Apple Health. All five connect to Apple Health in some way. Buzz Off doesn’t.
  • Caffeine in your body. HiCoffee, Caffeine App and NoCaffeine show a curve of the caffeine still in your body, and RECaf shows how much is left. Caffeine App and NoCaffeine also suggest when you may be ready to sleep. Buzz Off doesn’t estimate this.
  • Apple Watch. HiCoffee, Caffeine App and RECaf have Apple Watch apps.
  • Widgets. HiCoffee, Caffeine App and NoCaffeine offer widgets.
  • More devices. HiCoffee and RECaf run on iPad. HiCoffee and Stop Coffee run on Mac. HiCoffee, Caffeine App and Stop Coffee run on Apple Vision. Buzz Off is for iPhone.
  • Paying once. HiCoffee and Caffeine App offer a one-time unlock, and Stop Coffee a lifetime purchase. Buzz Off Pro is a subscription.

If you mainly want to know how much caffeine is in your body right now, or to log from your wrist, one of the trackers may suit you better.

Where Buzz Off fits

Buzz Off is built for one job: helping you quit or cut back. Choose Cold Turkey, with a day counter and the 21-Day Reset, or Gradual, where a weekly plan lowers your daily target step by step from what you drink now.

A check-in takes 2 or 3 taps: whether you had caffeine, the drink, your mood and any symptoms. Your progress shows as streaks, money saved, symptom trends and plan charts. Unlike the trackers, Buzz Off doesn’t estimate the caffeine in your body; it’s about changing the habit. It’s free to download, with an optional Pro subscription.

Not sure which way suits you? Our guide on how to quit caffeine compares stopping all at once with cutting back, and the taper planner shows what a gradual plan could look like.
