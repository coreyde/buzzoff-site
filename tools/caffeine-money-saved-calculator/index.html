How this is worked out

Everything here comes from what you enter: there are no averages or assumed prices. The tool takes the drinks you’d cut each day (all of them if you’re quitting) times the price of one, then multiplies by 7 for a week, by 365 for a year, and by 365 divided by 12 for a month.

The Buzz Off app shows your money saved too, based on what you tell it you usually spend.
