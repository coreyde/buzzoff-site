Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using Our Service, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify You. Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  • Email address
  • First name and last name
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Authentication information from Third‑Party Sign‑In Providers (for example, when You sign in with Apple or Google)

Health and Habit Data (User‑Entered)

The core purpose of Buzz Off is to help You reduce or quit caffeine. To provide this functionality, You may choose to enter the following types of data:

  • Daily check‑ins (mood, fatigue, headaches, irritability, cravings, low mood, and whether You had caffeine)
  • Caffeine intake logs (for example, type of drink and amount of caffeine)
  • Your goals and preferences (for example, Cold Turkey or Gradual mode, reasons for quitting, symptoms and goals selected during onboarding)
  • Estimated daily caffeine amount (for example, in milligrams per day)

This information is entered by You and is used to power features such as streaks, progress charts, achievements, insights, and tools designed to support Your caffeine reduction journey. We do not sell this data.

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service.

Usage Data may include information such as Your Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, crash information, in‑app events (for example, which screens You view and which features You use), and other diagnostic data that help Us understand how the Service is used and how it performs. We may also derive an approximate location (such as Your country and city) from Your IP address for analytics. We do not use this data for advertising, and We do not use it to track You across other companies' apps or websites.

This data is used to maintain and improve the stability, performance, and user experience of the Service.

Information from Third‑Party Sign‑In Providers

The Company allows You to create an account and log in to use the Service through the following Third‑Party Sign‑In Providers:

  • Apple
  • Google
  • Email and password (available in earlier versions of the App)

If You decide to register through or otherwise grant Us access to a Third‑Party Sign‑In Provider, We may collect Personal Data that is already associated with Your provider account, such as Your name and email address, in accordance with that provider’s own privacy policy.

You may also have the option of sharing additional information from Your provider account. If You choose to provide such information and Personal Data, You are giving the Company permission to use, share, and store it in a manner consistent with this Privacy Policy.

Information Collected while Using the Application

While using Our Application, in order to provide certain features of Our Application, We may collect, with Your prior permission:

Pictures and other information from Your Device's photo library (for example, when You choose to upload a profile photo).

We use this information only to provide features of Our Service, such as displaying Your chosen profile image. The information may be uploaded to the Company's servers and/or a Service Provider's server or it may be stored on Your Device.

You can enable or disable access to this information at any time through Your Device settings.

Push Notifications and Preferences

With Your consent, We may collect and use a push notification token associated with Your Device to send:

  • Daily reminders to check in and track Your progress
  • Encouragement messages and achievement alerts

You can change Your notification preferences or disable notifications at any time in the App settings or Your Device settings.

Use of Your Personal Data

The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Service, including to create and manage Your Account, process Your check‑ins, track Your progress, streaks, and achievements, and provide tools designed to support You in reducing or quitting caffeine.
  • To manage Your subscription, including verifying whether You have an active Pro subscription and unlocking Pro‑only features.
  • To contact You via in‑app messages or email, where appropriate (for example, important service updates or account‑related messages).
  • To provide notifications, including daily reminders and encouragement notifications, if You have opted in.
  • To improve the Service by monitoring usage, diagnosing crashes, and understanding how features are used so We can make improvements.
  • To comply with legal obligations and to protect Our rights, where required.

The Company does not sell Your Personal Data.

Third‑Party Service Providers

We may share Your information with Service Providers to monitor and analyze the use of our Service, to process subscriptions, to store data, or to contact You. These Service Providers act on Our behalf and are required to keep Your information secure.

  • Supabase – Provides authentication, database storage, and profile image storage for the App.
  • RevenueCat – Manages subscriptions and entitlements to determine whether You have access to Pro features.
  • Superwall – Displays paywalls and subscription offers inside the App. Superwall also receives the plan You chose (Cold Turkey or Gradual), when You finished setting up the App and how many days ago that was, and where You heard about Buzz Off. We use these to personalise and test Our subscription screens. They are removed from Your Device when You sign out.
  • Sentry – Collects crash reports and diagnostic information to help Us improve stability and fix bugs.
  • PostHog – Collects in‑app usage events (for example, screens viewed and features used) and an approximate location derived from Your IP address, linked to Your account ID, to help Us understand how the App is used and improve it.
  • Expo / EAS – Helps deliver app updates and manage over‑the‑air updates for the App.
  • Apple – Provides Sign in with Apple, App Store distribution, and in‑app purchase infrastructure.
  • Google – Provides Google sign‑in and related authentication infrastructure.
  • Resend – Sends transactional emails, such as account deletion confirmations.
  • Google Analytics – Collects anonymous usage data on Our website (for example, page views, traffic sources, and device type) to help Us understand how visitors find and use the landing page. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Each of these Service Providers has its own privacy practices. We encourage You to review their privacy policies where appropriate.

Data Retention

The Company will retain Your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

  • Account and profile data are retained while Your Account is active. See “Deleting Your Account” below for what happens when You delete it.
  • Daily check‑ins and caffeine logs are retained for as long as Your Account exists so that You can view historical progress, unless You delete Your Account.
  • Crash and diagnostic data are retained by Our analytics and error‑tracking providers for limited periods according to their policies.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete Your Account at any time in the App: Profile → Delete Account. If You haven't finished setting up, use the “Account” link at the top of the setup screens. When You delete Your Account, We immediately and permanently delete Your profile, Your check‑ins, caffeine logs and notes, Your achievements, reminders and notification settings, any feedback You sent from the App, Your profile photo and Your sign‑in account. If You signed in with Apple, We also ask Apple to disconnect Buzz Off from Your Apple ID (in version 2.0.5 and later of the App). We then send a confirmation to Your email address.

We also ask Our Service Providers to delete their copies. RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions, deletes Your customer record. Superwall, which shows Our subscription screens, deletes Your data 25 days after Our request, keeping only anonymous revenue records.

What deleting doesn't do. Deleting Your Account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in iOS Settings → Your name → Subscriptions, or Apple will keep charging You. Apple keeps its own records of Your purchases.

What We keep. Some copies stay for a limited time and are then removed automatically:

  • Analytics events (PostHog) and error reports (Sentry), which are linked to Your account ID, until each provider's retention period ends.
  • Email delivery logs (Resend).
  • Our database backups, until they roll off.

Messages You sent to Our support inbox are kept so We can answer support questions. To ask about any of this, email support@buzzoffapp.com.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on Your location, You may have certain rights regarding Your Personal Data. Regardless of where You live, We provide the following controls:

  • Access and update – You can view and update certain profile information directly in the App.
  • Delete Your Account – You can delete Your Account at any time in the App (Profile → Delete Account). See “Deleting Your Account” above for what is deleted and what is kept.
  • Export – Certain parts of Your data (for example, historical check‑ins) can be exported from within the App if You are a Pro subscriber.
  • Notifications – You can enable or disable push notifications in the App settings or in Your Device settings.

If You are a parent or guardian and You become aware that Your child has provided Us with Personal Data, please contact Us. If We become aware that We have collected Personal Data from a child under 13 without verification of parental consent, We will take steps to remove that information from Our servers.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
