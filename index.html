Quit or cut back on caffeine, at your own pace.

Buzz Off is an iPhone app for going cold turkey or cutting back step by step. Free to download, with an optional Pro subscription.

For iPhone, in English

Two ways to do it

Pick the one that suits you. You can switch later.

Cold Turkey

Stop now and count the days

A day counter shows how long you’ve been caffeine free, and the 21-Day Reset gives your first three weeks some structure.

Gradual

Cut back step by step

Start from what you usually have. A weekly plan lowers your daily target a little each week, and you log against today’s target.

How a day works

A few taps a day.

  1. Check in

    A few taps: caffeine yes or no, the drink, your mood and, if you like, any symptoms.

  2. Log a drink

    Quick-add coffee, tea, energy drinks and more, with the caffeine in each.

  3. Watch it add up

    Your day counter, streaks and money saved, based on what you usually spend.

  4. Get a nudge

    A daily reminder at the time that suits you.

Gradual plan

A plan that starts from you

Week 1 is for tracking and learning. From week 2, your daily target steps down a little each week. In week 6 you hold steady or adjust.

Example: starting from 300 mg a day
WeekDaily target
Week 1 Track and learn300 mg
Week 2 Step down255 mg
Week 3 Step down217 mg
Week 4 Step down184 mg
Week 5 Step down157 mg
Week 6 Maintain or adjust133 mg

The same rule the app uses. Your plan starts from your own number.

Slip-ups are data, not failure

Had a coffee you didn’t plan? Log it, notice the pattern and keep going. Your mood and symptom trends show how things change for you.

Questions

Is Buzz Off free?

Buzz Off is free to download. Buzz Off Pro is an optional subscription that adds longer progress history, the full achievement set, extra reminders, the full Gradual plan timeline, plan updates, recovery support after a slip and data export. The plans and prices on offer are shown in the app before you buy.

Should I go cold turkey or cut back gradually?

Buzz Off supports both, and you can change modes later in Edit Profile. Cold Turkey counts your caffeine-free days and follows the 21-Day Reset. Gradual starts from what you usually have and lowers your daily target a little each week.

Does Buzz Off work on iPad or Android?

No. Buzz Off is an iPhone app, and it’s in English.

How do I cancel Buzz Off Pro?

Any time, in your App Store account settings: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to stop the next renewal. Apple’s guide: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428

Is Buzz Off medical advice?

No. Buzz Off is a tracking and habit tool, not medical advice. Everyone’s different: if you have a health condition, are pregnant or take medication, talk to your doctor before changing how much caffeine you have.
