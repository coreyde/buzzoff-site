About Buzz Off

Buzz Off is an iPhone app for quitting or cutting back on caffeine, at your own pace. It’s made by Corey De Ponte, its solo founder.

Why I built it

I used to drink three or more coffees a day. I was exhausted most of the time, but I still couldn’t stay asleep at night.

I tried going cold turkey first. The headaches in those first few days sent me straight back to coffee, so I cut back gradually instead. Today I don’t drink caffeine at all.

The hardest part was giving up that hot coffee first thing in the morning. Decaf and herbal teas got me through it, and they’re still how I start the day.

Before Buzz Off I tried a few other apps. None of them stuck. I’d stop logging after a while and slowly slide back into old habits. So I built the app I wanted: quick to log, easy to keep up, and made for cutting back slowly as well as stopping outright.

I hope it helps you too.

What Buzz Off is

Two ways to do it: Cold Turkey, with a day counter and the 21-Day Reset, or Gradual, with a weekly plan that lowers your daily target from your own starting point. A short daily check-in, quick-add drink logging, streaks, money saved and symptom trends sit around both.

The short reads in the app list their sources. Buzz Off is a tracking and habit tool, not medical advice, and it has no ads.
