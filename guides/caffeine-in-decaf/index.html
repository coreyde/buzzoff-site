How much caffeine is in decaf coffee?

By Corey De Ponte, founder of Buzz Off · Last reviewed: 26 November 2026

Short answer

Decaf coffee isn’t caffeine-free: the FDA says it typically has 2 to 15 mg in an 8-fluid-ounce cup (FDA). That’s far less than regular brewed coffee, which the FDA puts at 113 to 247 mg per 12 fluid ounces (FDA).

What studies of coffee-shop decaf found

  • Ten decaf coffees bought from different coffee shops contained 0 to 13.9 mg of caffeine per 16-oz serving (McCusker et al., 2006).
  • Decaf espresso bought from the same outlet on different occasions ranged from 3.0 to 15.8 mg per shot, and decaf brewed coffee from 12.0 to 13.4 mg per 16-oz serving (McCusker et al., 2006).
  • An earlier study found coffees sold as decaf had less than 17.7 mg of caffeine per serving (McCusker et al., 2003).

Can decaf add up?

For most people, decaf is a big step down. At the FDA’s upper figure of 15 mg per 8-fluid-ounce cup (FDA), five cups of decaf would be about 75 mg, less than a single 200 ml cup of filter coffee at about 90 mg (EFSA).

It matters more if you’ve been told to avoid caffeine altogether. The authors of the decaf study note that some medical conditions and medicines call for caffeine-restricted diets, and that people who need to avoid caffeine should know decaf may contain it (McCusker et al., 2006). If that’s you, talk to your doctor or pharmacist.

Decaf when you’re quitting

Many people use decaf to keep the taste and ritual of coffee while they cut back or quit. If you want zero caffeine, herbal teas are the safer bet: USDA lists 0 mg for brewed chamomile tea (USDA).

Questions

Does decaf coffee have caffeine?

Yes, a little. The FDA says decaf typically has 2 to 15 mg in an 8-fluid-ounce cup (FDA).

How much caffeine is in a decaf espresso?

In one study, decaf espresso from the same outlet ranged from 3.0 to 15.8 mg per shot (McCusker et al., 2006).

Is decaf OK if I’m quitting caffeine?

Many people use it to keep the ritual. It’s far lower in caffeine than regular coffee (FDA), but not zero. If you’ve been told to avoid caffeine completely, ask your doctor.

How many cups of decaf equal one regular coffee?

At the FDA’s upper figure of 15 mg per 8-fluid-ounce decaf (FDA), it would take about six cups to match a 200 ml filter coffee at about 90 mg (EFSA).
