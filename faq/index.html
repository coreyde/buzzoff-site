Pricing and Pro

Is Buzz Off free?

Buzz Off is free to download, with an optional Pro subscription. The plans and prices on offer are shown in the app before you buy, and prices vary by country.

What does Buzz Off Pro add?

Longer progress history, the full achievement set, extra reminders, the full Gradual plan timeline, plan updates, recovery support after a slip and data export.

Is there a free trial?

When a free trial is on offer, the app shows it, and how long it lasts, before you subscribe.

How do I cancel Pro?

Any time, in your App Store account settings: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to stop the next renewal. See Apple’s guide to cancelling a subscription.

I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back?

Open the app’s subscription settings and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID you subscribed with. If that doesn’t work, contact us and we’ll help.

Cold Turkey and Gradual

Should I go cold turkey or cut back gradually?

Buzz Off supports both. Cold Turkey counts your caffeine-free days and follows the 21-Day Reset. Gradual starts from what you usually have and lowers your daily target a little each week. Pick the one you think you’ll stick with.

Can I switch modes later?

Yes. You can change between Cold Turkey and Gradual in Edit Profile. Switching to Gradual asks for your starting point and starts a new plan.

How does the Gradual plan work?

The plan runs for six weeks from your usual daily amount. Week 1 is for tracking and learning, at your usual amount. From week 2, each week’s target is about 15% lower than the week before, and it never goes below 25 mg. In week 6 you hold steady or adjust. Each day you log against that day’s target.

What if I slip up?

Log it and keep going. A slip-up is useful information about what tripped you up, not a failure. Pro adds recovery support after a slip.

Devices and features

Does Buzz Off work on iPad or Android?

No. Buzz Off is an iPhone app.

What languages is it in?

English only, for now.

Does it work with Apple Health, widgets or Apple Watch?

Not at the moment. Buzz Off keeps your check-ins, drinks and progress in the app itself.

How do I log a drink?

Quick-add coffee, tea, energy drinks and more, with the caffeine in each. Your daily check-in also asks whether you had caffeine and what you drank.

Privacy and your data

Does Buzz Off show ads?

No. There are no ads in Buzz Off.

Do you sell my data?

No. Our privacy policy explains what the app collects, why, and who helps us run it.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time in the app: Profile, then Delete Account. That permanently deletes your profile, check-ins, caffeine logs and progress; the privacy policy lists exactly what is deleted.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel that in Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Can I export my data?

Yes, with Pro. Data export is in your profile.

Health

Is Buzz Off medical advice?

No. Buzz Off is a tracking and habit tool, not medical advice. Everyone’s different: if you have a health condition, are pregnant or take medication, talk to your doctor before changing how much caffeine you have.
